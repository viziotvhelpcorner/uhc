How to Activate Your UHC Card?
-------------------------------------------------
Use these simple steps to activate your UHC card online:

-Start your mobile phone, tablet, or computer and open any internet browser.
-Visit the official activation website: activate.uhc.com
Type your UHC Member ID exactly as shown on your insurance card.
Enter your date of birth in the required format.
Fill in your ZIP code for verification.
Press the Continue button to move ahead.
Complete any extra steps shown on the screen.
Once finished, your UHC card will be ready to use successfully.
